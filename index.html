EVERY REP EARNS RANK

RankTime is an iPhone interval timer for guided workouts, with color cues, audio, haptics, camera rep counting, heart rate tracking, and a military service record that never resets.

› Interval training

Build templates with exercises, rounds, and durations. Time or reps mode, mixed or continuous order.

› Camera rep counting

Record your set while on-device Vision body pose tracking counts reps and paints live overlays.

› Heart rate

Live heart rate from a Polar H10, Apple Watch, or any Bluetooth chest strap, with five color zones.

› Apple Health

Completed workouts and heart rate samples save straight to Apple Health, with your permission.

› iCloud sync

Presets, history, and body metrics sync privately across your devices through your own iCloud account.

› Statistics

Track volume, calories, and progress per exercise over time, all stored on device.
