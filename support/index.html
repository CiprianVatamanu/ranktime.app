Frequently asked questions

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Workouts, health data, and recordings stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which heart rate sensors work?

Polar H10 and standard Bluetooth heart rate straps.

How does rep counting work?

The camera follows your body with Apple Vision body pose tracking, on the phone itself. Nothing is uploaded. Nine exercises count straight away: push-ups, pull-ups, squats, burpees, kettlebell swings, snatches, sit-ups, lunges and jumping jacks. Teach it any other exercise in the Trainer with 20 seconds of your own reps. With a Polar H10 strap, you can pick the strap to count an exercise instead.

Where do I put the phone?

On the floor or a tripod, where the camera sees all of you. Front, side or behind all work, and you do not need to line anything up first. Each exercise in the Trainer says which side suits it best. The camera follows one person, so train with nobody else in the picture.

I deleted a built-in exercise. Can I get it back?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Reinstall built-in exercises. Every built-in you deleted comes back, and your reps and workouts stay with it.

What if I do a different exercise?

Only the exercise of the set counts. Do something else and the app tells you what it saw instead of counting it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Sync uses your own iCloud account on the device.

How do I turn analytics off?

Analytics are off by default. You can review the setting anytime in the app under Settings.
