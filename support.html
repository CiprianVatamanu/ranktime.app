Frequently asked questions

Does my data leave my phone?

No. Workouts, health data, and recordings stay on your device and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which heart rate sensors work?

Polar H10, Apple Watch through Apple Health, and standard Bluetooth heart rate straps.

How does rep counting work?

When you record a set, the app uses on-device Apple Vision body pose tracking to count reps. Nothing is uploaded.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Sync uses your own iCloud account on the device.

How do I turn analytics off?

Analytics are off by default. You can review the setting anytime in the app under Settings.
