Thrusters: How to Do Them With a Barbell, Dumbbells or Kettlebells

Updated November 28, 2026 · 15 minute read · Free PDF

A thruster is a front squat and an overhead press in one move: you squat with the weight at your shoulders, and the drive out of the bottom carries straight on into pressing it overhead. You can do thrusters with a barbell, dumbbells or kettlebells. They are one of CrossFit's signature lifts, in Fran, one of its best known benchmark workouts, and in every CrossFit Open from 2011 to 2022. Here is how to do one, which weight to use, the muscles it works, why it feels so hard, what a good Fran time is, and a 6 week plan.

How do you do a thruster?

In one smooth movement, in this order. The steps follow CrossFit's own description of the thruster (CrossFit, 2019).

  1. Set up. Feet about shoulder width apart, toes turned out a little. Rest the bar high on the front of your shoulders, hands just outside them in a full grip, elbows up in front. Dumbbells rest on your shoulders. A kettlebell rests on the back of your forearm, your fist by your chin.
  2. Squat. Push your hips back and down until they are below your knees. Your heels stay down, your knees stay in line with your feet, and your back stays flat.
  3. Drive. Stand up fast, straightening your hips and knees hard. Keep the weight on your shoulders: your legs, not your arms, start it moving up.
  4. Press. As the weight pops off your shoulders and passes your chin, press it straight up with your arms, without a pause.
  5. Finish. Arms straight overhead, the weight over your ankles, hips and knees straight, back flat. That is one rep.
  6. Back down. Bring the weight all the way back to your shoulders, then go straight into the next squat.

In Fran and the CrossFit Open, a rep counts when the crease of your hip passes below the top of your knees, and ends with your knees, hips and arms straight and the bar overhead (Mangine and colleagues, 2022). In Fran, each set starts with the bar on the floor, so clean it to your shoulders first. To warm up, CrossFit suggests 5 to 7 push presses with a pause overhead, 5 to 7 front squats with a pause at the bottom, then 5 to 7 thrusters.

Can you do thrusters with dumbbells or kettlebells?

Yes. The movement is the same, but each one changes where the weight sits and what works hardest.

BarbellDumbbellsKettlebells
Rests onFront of the shouldersThe shouldersThe forearms
Heaviest pressYesAbout 7% lessNot measured
Press works moreTricepsFront shouldersNot measured

In a standing press, 15 men lifted about 7% less with dumbbells than with a barbell. The dumbbells worked the front of their shoulders about 15% harder, the barbell their triceps about 39% harder (Saeterbakken and Fimland, 2013). No study has compared kettlebell thrusters. If a barbell makes your elbows hit your knees at the bottom of the squat, CrossFit suggests dumbbells or kettlebells instead (CrossFit, 2019). With one dumbbell or one kettlebell, one side works at a time: switch hands each set.

What weight should you use for thrusters?

Lighter than you think. These are the weights in CrossFit's best known thruster workouts, made for trained athletes:

WorkoutMenWomen
Fran, barbell95 lb (43 kg)65 lb (29 kg)
CrossFit Open, barbell95 to 100 lb (43 to 45 kg)65 lb (29 kg)
Open 22.3, last round135 lb (61 kg)85 lb (39 kg)
Open 20.2, dumbbells50 lb (23 kg)35 lb (16 kg)

In 15 men with at least 3 years of CrossFit, Fran's 43 kg was about half (49%) of their heaviest single thruster, which was 89 kg in the middle of the group, and the most they could do in a row with it was about 30 (Leitão and colleagues, 2021). So pick your weight by the set in front of you: for sets of 10 or more, a weight you could lift a few more times than the set asks; for heavy sets of 5, one you could lift about 8 times. For Fran, the plan below uses a weight you can do 21 times in a row when you are fresh.

What muscles do thrusters work?

Mostly your thighs, glutes, shoulders and triceps, with your trunk holding you steady. No study has measured muscle activity in the thruster itself: we searched the research on 28 November 2026 and found none. But it is two lifts that have been studied, a front squat and an overhead press.

Thighs and glutesThe squat half. Your quadriceps straighten your knees and your glutes straighten your hips as you drive up (OpenStax). In 15 trained people, the front squat worked the muscles as much as the back squat, with less compression at the knee (Gullett and colleagues, 2009)
ShouldersThe press half. Your deltoids lift your arms overhead, and your upper trapezius turns your shoulder blades up. In a standing press, dumbbells worked the front of the shoulders about 15% harder than a barbell (Saeterbakken and Fimland, 2013)
TricepsThey straighten your elbows at the top. The barbell worked them about 39% harder than dumbbells in the same study
Abs and backThey hold you upright with the weight in front of you and then overhead. In 7 trained men, the front squat worked the back muscles beside the spine harder than a back extension hold did, while a plank worked the abs harder than the front squat (Bautista and colleagues, 2020)
Lats and bicepsLittle. They are pulling muscles, and a thruster only pushes. Fran pairs thrusters with pull-ups, which train them

Is a thruster the same as a squat to press?

Nearly. Squat to press is another name for it, used mostly with dumbbells, and some trainers mean a slower version by it: stand up, then press. In a thruster there is no stop: the drive of your legs lifts the weight off your shoulders, and your arms take over as it passes your chin (CrossFit, 2019). Researchers describe it the same way, a squat that flows into a press without pausing (Rios and colleagues, 2025).

Thruster vs push press

A push press starts standing: a short dip of the knees, then a drive that pops the weight overhead. A thruster starts from a full squat, so it is far more work with the same bar. By the standard estimates of a 2026 compendium, the bar travels 1.30 meters (4.3 feet) in a thruster by a man of average size, against 0.71 meters (2.3 feet) in his push press, and the thruster lifts most of his body out of the squat as well. That makes one thruster with Fran's bar about three times the work of a push press with it (Mangine and colleagues, 2026).

Kettlebell thruster vs clean and press

A kettlebell clean and press brings the bell up from between your legs to your shoulder on every rep, with the same hip snap as kettlebell swings, then presses it overhead, with no squat. A thruster keeps the bell at your shoulder and adds a full squat. RankTime's camera counts both, as CLEAN & PRESS and THRUSTERS.

Are thrusters a good exercise?

Yes, for strength and fitness at once, with two limits. What the research says:

Full bodyFor pushing, yes. Your legs, hips, shoulders, triceps and trunk all work. The muscles that pull do not, so pair thrusters with a pull, as Fran does
CardioYes, at Fran's pace. In 25 trained CrossFitters, Fran drove heart rate to 95% of maximum, vigorous exercise by the American College of Sports Medicine's thresholds, the researchers note (Rios and colleagues, 2025). In 20 others, 62% of Fran's energy came from aerobic metabolism, even though it lasts only minutes (Rios and colleagues, 2024)
MuscleLikely, as with any weight training. No study has trained people with thrusters alone. The American College of Sports Medicine's 2026 review found that light and heavy loads build muscle alike when the last reps of each set are hard, with 10 or more sets a week for each muscle group (Currier and colleagues, 2026)
Belly fatNot from one spot. No exercise burns fat where it works: 6 weeks of ab exercises cut no belly fat in inactive adults (Vispute and colleagues, 2011). What counts is your total activity over weeks: for weight loss, the American College of Sports Medicine advises 150 to 250 minutes of moderate activity a week for a modest loss, and more than 250 for more (Donnelly and colleagues, 2009)

Why are thrusters so hard?

Because every rep is two lifts and a lot of work, and a set leaves you out of breath fast. What the studies measured:

The workOne thruster with Fran's bar is about three times the work of a push press: the bar travels from the bottom of your squat to above your head, 1.30 meters (4.3 feet) for a man of average size, and you lift about 88% of your body weight out of the squat too (Mangine and colleagues, 2026)
Heart and lungsIn 15 trained men, Fran pushed heart rate and blood lactate about as high as an all-out 2,000 meter row: 182 against 183 beats a minute at the peak, and 12.6 against 14.4 mmol/L of lactate (Leitão and colleagues, 2021)
The burn buildsAcross Fran's three rounds, the share of energy from the fast system that makes lactate rose from 18% to 48% (Rios and colleagues, 2023)
It lastsAfter Fran, trained CrossFitters jumped 8% lower and held a plank 46% shorter, and recovery can take more than 24 hours (Rios and colleagues, 2024)

With dumbbells, each arm steadies its own weight: in the press study above, the shoulders worked harder with dumbbells even though they moved less weight.

What is a good Fran time?

Fran is 21, 15 and 9 reps of thrusters and pull-ups, for time: 21 thrusters, 21 pull-ups, 15 and 15, then 9 and 9. The bar weighs 95 lb (43 kg) for men and 65 lb (29 kg) for women. Like the Cindy workout, it is one of CrossFit's "Girl" benchmark workouts, done the same way every time to measure progress (Meier and colleagues, 2021). These are the best times that 10,000 people reported on their CrossFit Games profiles in 2017 (Mangine and colleagues, 2018):

Top 20%MiddleSlowest 20%
Men 18 to 342:464:105:34
Women 18 to 343:075:317:54
Men 35 and over3:215:117:01
Women 35 and over4:196:087:57

So 4 to 6 minutes is typical, and under 3 minutes is fast. Measured in the lab, 15 men with at least 3 years of CrossFit took 4:02 in the middle of the group (Leitão and colleagues, 2021), and 25 who trained at least five times a week averaged 2:57 for the men and 3:26 for the women (Rios and colleagues, 2025). In a 2022 study, the slowest quarter took 7 to 11 minutes and more, most likely because they could not do many thrusters or pull-ups in a row, the researchers wrote (Mangine and colleagues, 2022).

What is a thruster workout?

Any workout built on thrusters, usually paired with a pull or a jump and done against the clock. CrossFit put thrusters in its Open every year from 2011 to 2022, in 12 of its 60 workouts (Mangine and colleagues, 2023). The best known:

WorkoutWhat you do
Fran21, 15 and 9 thrusters and pull-ups, for time
Open 14.5 and 16.521, 18, 15, 12, 9, 6 and 3 thrusters and burpees, for time
Open 15.527, 21, 15 and 9 calories of rowing and thrusters, for time
Open 11.6, 12.5 and 18.57 minutes: 3 thrusters and 3 chest-to-bar pull-ups, then 6 and 6, adding 3 each round
Open 20.220 minutes: 4 dumbbell thrusters, 6 toes-to-bar, 24 double-unders, as many rounds as you can

The barbell ones use 95 lb (43 kg) for men and 65 lb (29 kg) for women, 100 lb (45 kg) for men in 11.6, 12.5 and 18.5. Fran, 14.5 and 15.5 are descending ladders, each set smaller than the last: see our guide to the ladder workout. 20.2 is an AMRAP, as many rounds as possible before the clock runs out. Any of them works with two kettlebells or dumbbells instead of a bar.

How do you get better at thrusters?

Fix the common mistakes first, then train the two things that go with a fast Fran. The mistakes are CrossFit's own list (CrossFit, 2019):

Pressing too earlyYour arms push before your hips and knees are straight. Let your legs lift the weight off your shoulders. CrossFit's drill: 2 sets of 2 push presses, then 1 thruster
Squatting too soonYou start the next squat before the weight is back on your shoulders. Lower it all the way first, a little slower
Heels and kneesYour heels lift, or your knees cave in at the bottom. Keep your whole foot down and your knees in line with your feet
Elbows on kneesWith a barbell, your elbows hit your knees in the squat. Switch to dumbbells or kettlebells
Rounded low backYour low back rounds at the bottom. Keep it flat all the way down, and go lighter if it rounds before your hips pass your knees

Then train for it. In the 15 men of the Fran study, the most thrusters they could do in a row at Fran's weight was the strongest link with a fast Fran. Their heaviest single thruster, the most pull-ups they could do in a row and their 2,000 meter row time were linked too, less strongly (Leitão and colleagues, 2021). So train long sets at your workout weight and heavier sets of 5, as the plan below does.

How many thrusters should you do?

For strength, 2 to 3 sets, two or more days a week. The American College of Sports Medicine's 2026 review of 137 reviews found that strength grows with 2 or more sessions a week and 2 to 3 sets an exercise, and muscle with 10 or more sets a week for each muscle group. Light loads built muscle as well as heavy ones, though heavy loads built more strength, and sets did not need to go to failure (Currier and colleagues, 2026). Fran itself is 45 thrusters.

Ladders are thrusters and pull-ups in turn at your Fran weight, the one you can do 21 times in a row when fresh: 12-9-6 means 12 thrusters, 12 pull-ups, 9 and 9, then 6 and 6, as fast as you can with good reps. The heavier day is 5 sets of 5 thrusters with a weight you could lift about 8 times, with 2 to 3 minutes of rest: see how long to rest between sets. Common questions

Are thrusters an Olympic lift?

No. Olympic weightlifting has two lifts, the snatch and the clean and jerk (Meier and colleagues, 2021). But they are close: in 73 CrossFit athletes, the heaviest thruster closely tracked the heaviest clean and jerk (Oliver-López and colleagues, 2025).

Are thrusters push or pull?

Push: a squat and an overhead press, both pushing away from the floor. Balance them with a pull, such as pull-ups or rows.

Can I do thrusters every day?

You can, but you do not need to, and hard sets need recovery. When the total number of sets was the same, training a muscle once a week or more than 5 days a week built the same muscle (Currier and colleagues, 2026). After Fran, recovery can take more than 24 hours (Rios and colleagues, 2024). Two thruster days a week, as in the plan, leave room to recover.

Are thrusters a good substitute for wall balls?

Close. A wall ball shot is the same squat, then a throw of a medicine ball to a target instead of a press: in the CrossFit Open, a 20 lb (9 kg) ball to a 10 foot (3 meter) target for men, and 14 lb (6 kg) to 9 feet (2.7 meters) for women (Mangine and colleagues, 2023). Thrusters train the same legs and shoulders, but the weight never leaves your hands, so you can go heavier.
