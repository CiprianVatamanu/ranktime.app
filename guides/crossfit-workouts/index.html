CrossFit Workouts: Murph and Cindy

2 guides, each with a free PDF

A benchmark workout is a fixed workout you repeat to measure your fitness over time. Two of the best known are built on pull-ups, push-ups and squats with your own body weight: Murph and Cindy. These guides cover where they come from, how to scale them, how to pace them, and what a good result is.

All Bodyweight Kettlebell Isometric holds HIIT CrossFit Training basics

The two workouts at a glance

MurphA 1 mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 push-ups, 300 air squats and another 1 mile run, traditionally in a 20 lb (9 kg) vest. Most people take 45 to 75 minutes
CindyAs many rounds as you can in 20 minutes of 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 air squats. 10 to 15 rounds is solid fitness, over 20 is very fit

Where to start

  • Start with Cindy. It lasts 20 minutes whatever happens, and every round tells you how your pull-ups hold up.
  • Then plan your Murph. Split the reps into small rounds, go easy on the first mile, and scale the pull-ups or the vest until you can finish.
