Kettlebell Workouts

3 guides, each with a free PDF

Kettlebell training starts with one bell and one movement, the hip hinge: hips back with a flat back, then hips forward to stand. Learn it slowly and heavy with the deadlift, speed it up with the swing, then take the bell overhead with the snatch. Each guide covers the technique, the muscles it works, which bell to use, workouts and a test.

Learn them in this order

  1. Kettlebell deadlift. The same hinge done slowly, so you can learn it under a heavy bell. StrongFirst's coaches teach it first.
  2. Kettlebell swing. The hinge made fast: your hips snap the bell to chest height while your arms just hold on.
  3. Kettlebell snatch. A swing that keeps going: one arm, from between your legs to straight overhead. Learn the one arm swing first.

The three lifts at a glance

DeadliftFloor to standing with both hands on one bell. Glutes, hamstrings, thighs, back and grip. Start with a bell you can lift at least 8 times, but no more than 12. Test: the Swedish soldiers' minute with two 40 kg bells
SwingTo chest height, driven by the hips. Glutes and hamstrings, with the back, core and grip. Most beginners start with 8 to 12 kg, people who already lift with 16 kg
SnatchOne arm, overhead in one movement. Glutes, hamstrings, back, shoulders and grip. The classic test is 100 snatches in 5 minutes, with 24 kg for most men and 12 to 16 kg for most women

Your deadlift bell is usually your heaviest. If you are buying one bell for swings, pick the heavier of the two you are deciding between: you will grow into it within weeks.
