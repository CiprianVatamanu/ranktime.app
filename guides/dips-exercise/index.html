Dips Exercise: Bench, Bar or Ring Dips and How to Do Them

Updated November 22, 2026 · 12 minute read · Free PDF

A dip lowers your body between two bars by bending your elbows, then presses you back up until your arms are straight. It works your triceps, your chest and the front of your shoulders, and on bars it lifts nearly all of your weight. A 2022 study that filmed bench, bar and ring dips found that bar dips worked those muscles harder than bench dips, while bench dips took the shoulder past its own range, so the researchers advise leaving bench dips out of regular training. Learn the dip on bars instead, with your feet, a band or a dip machine helping until you can do 8 clean reps on your own. Men who dip every week did about 25 in one set. Here is which dip to pick, how to do one, how low to go, how to build up from zero, how many is good, and a 6 week plan.

Bench, bar or ring dips: which one?

In 2022, sports scientists at Southern Cross University in Australia filmed 13 experienced men doing four reps of each dip with 3D motion capture, and measured nine muscles with sensors on the skin (McKenzie and colleagues, 2022). A bench dip has your hands on a bench behind you and your feet on the floor. A bar dip has your hands on two bars and your feet off the floor. A ring dip is the same on two hanging rings, which move. Nobody told the men how to dip, so each did it his own way.

The bar dip worked the triceps, the chest, the front of the shoulders, the lats and the muscles that hold the shoulder blades harder than the bench dip: peak triceps activity was 1.04 millivolts on the bars against 0.83 on the bench. The ring dip matched the bar dip and worked the chest, the lats and the biceps harder still. The bench dip took the arms furthest behind the body: 101% of the most each man reached in a separate test, against 88% on the bars and 69% on the rings.

That is why the authors conclude that bench dips should not be part of regular strength training or rehab: other exercises work the triceps as hard without that strain, such as triangle (diamond) push-ups and triceps kickbacks. They call the bar dip the next step up from the bench, and the ring dip the best for the chest, unless a fall is a worry, as it is for beginners and for anyone tired.

How to do a dip on bars

  1. Grip. Hands about shoulder width apart, palms facing each other. On a V-shaped dip station, hold the bars where they are narrow, not at the wide end.
  2. Get up. Jump or step up until your arms are straight and your body hangs still. Pull your shoulders down, away from your ears. Bend your knees and cross your ankles if your feet would touch the floor.
  3. Lower. Bend your elbows and let your chest tip a little forward. Your elbows point back, not out to the sides. Go down under control, never drop.
  4. Stop at the bottom. When your elbows are bent to about a right angle and your upper arms are about level with the floor.
  5. Press up. Push the bars away until your arms are straight again. That is one rep: straight arms to straight arms.
  6. Breathe. In on the way down, out on the way up.

How low should you go?

A dips test counts a rep when your elbows bend to at least a right angle and you press back up to straight arms (Topend Sports). That is about where your upper arms are level with the floor. Going deeper tires you sooner, and the same depth on every rep keeps your numbers comparable from week to week.

The bottom is also where the shoulder works hardest. In the bench, bar and ring study, the men chose their own depth and lowered on the bars until their arms were 88% of the way to the most they could reach behind them. The researchers point to that position, the arms furthest behind the body, as the dip's most vulnerable one for the front of the shoulder. Nothing is gained by sinking further than a right angle: the rep already counts.

Chest dips vs tricep dips

Lean forward with your elbows a little out and a bar dip is usually called a chest dip. Stay upright with your elbows close to your sides and it is called a triceps dip. Both work the triceps and the chest. What the lean changes has not been tested on its own, but the two studies show three things.

More lean, more chest?Bar and ring dips leaned the upper body much further forward than the bench dip, which leaned about 13 degrees, and both worked the chest harder. The feet and the equipment changed too, so the lean is only part of it
Experienced dippers leanMen who dip every week leaned their upper body about 37 degrees forward at the bottom of a bar dip, done their own way
The lower chest is unprovenThe 2022 study measured the upper part of the chest muscle only, so it cannot say whether dips favour the lower chest, as is often claimed

So dip with the small forward lean that comes naturally on bars. For the most chest work, the ring dip led all three. For the triceps alone, the researchers point to triangle push-ups and kickbacks.

Dips muscles worked

A dip straightens your elbows and brings your arms down and forward against your own weight. The bench, bar and ring study measured nine muscles:

TricepsStraighten your elbows: the main mover. About 25% more work on bars and rings than on the bench
ChestThe pectoralis major presses you up out of the bottom. Most on rings, then bars, least on the bench
Front of the shouldersThe front deltoids help the chest press. More on bars and rings than on the bench
Shoulder blade musclesThe serratus anterior and the upper trapezius hold your shoulder blades in place. More on bars and rings
LatsSteady your body over your hands. Most on rings
BicepsSteady your elbows on the rings, which worked them harder than bars or the bench

The work grows as a set goes on. In a second study from the same team, 15 men did one set of bar dips until they could do no more. In the last reps their chest, triceps, lats and shoulder blade muscles worked harder than in the first, while each rep slowed from about 1.4 to 2.2 seconds (McKenzie and colleagues, 2022, fatigue study).

Do dips hurt your shoulders?

Dips have a name for sore shoulders. Here is what the research shows, and the rules that follow from it.

  • The strain sits at the bottom. The front of the shoulder is loaded most when the arms are furthest behind the body. Bench dips went past each man's own range there, bar dips used most of it, ring dips the least.
  • Chest tears are rare. The researchers found two published cases of a torn chest tendon from bar dips. Another case report describes a 26 year old CrossFit athlete who tore his in a ring dip. After surgery and 16 weeks of rehab, he was back to his sport (da Silva and colleagues, 2022).
  • Weight, fatigue and grip raise the risk. Extra weight, tired muscles or a wide grip may raise the risk of a chest tear markedly, the researchers write.
  • Tired reps keep their shape. In the fatigue study, the last reps went no deeper than the first. Its authors still advise a spotter or a lower bar for sets to exhaustion, and not going to exhaustion too often, because a tired dipper can fall.

How to go easy on your shoulders

  1. Leave bench dips out of regular training. Train the triceps with triangle push-ups instead.
  2. Stop when your upper arms are about level with the floor.
  3. Keep your hands about shoulder width apart, never wide.
  4. Add weight only after 3 sets of 12 clean reps, and a little at a time.
  5. Go to exhaustion rarely, and on bars low enough to step down from.
  6. Learn on bars, not rings, and stay on bars when you are tired.
  7. Pain at the front of the shoulder or in the chest is a stop sign. See a doctor or physiotherapist before you dip again.

Can't do a dip yet? Build up in five steps

The bar dip lifts nearly all of your weight. These steps take some of it off, from the most help to none. The first three are the ones the bench, bar and ring researchers suggest before full bar dips.

1. Standing bar dipsBars low enough that your feet stay on the floor. Your legs take as much of your weight as you need. For complete beginners
2. Machine dipsAn assisted dip machine: you kneel on a pad and a counterweight lifts part of you. Take less help as you get stronger. Useful at every stage
3. Band dipsA long resistance band across the bars, under your knees. It helps most at the bottom, where you need it, and asks for more balance than a machine
4. Bar dipsYour own weight, in sets of 8 to 12
5. Weighted or ring dipsWeight hung from a dip belt at your waist, or rings for the most chest work once you are steady

When to move up: work in sets of 8 to 12 reps, the American College of Sports Medicine's range for beginners (ACSM, 2009). When you can do 3 sets of 12 clean reps, take the next step, or less help, and start again at 8.

How many dips should you be able to do?

No study has published dip standards for the general public. These are the best numbers there are.

Lifters who log their sets. Strength Level ranks lifters on 517,475 dip results that its users logged from 2016 to 2026, 489,613 by men and 27,862 by women. The middle, stronger than half of them, is 20 dips for men and 9 for women.

These are people who train and log it, so the average person can do fewer. In the fatigue study, 15 men who did dips every week, with about 4 years of strength training, managed 25 in one set on average, between Strength Level's intermediate and advanced men.

The records. Simon Kent of the UK did 3,989 parallel bar dips in one hour in 1998. Pascal Fankhauser of Switzerland did 66 ring dips in one minute in January 2026, and Ruslan Fatakhov of Kazakhstan did 94 bar dips in one minute carrying an 18 kg (40 lb) pack in 2023 (Guinness World Records).

Test yourself after a warm up: one set of clean bar dips, elbows to a right angle and back to straight arms, until you cannot do another. Then let the number pick your training:

Fewer than 8Train with help: steps 1 to 3 above
8 or moreSets of bar dips, 8 to 12 reps
3 sets of 12Add weight with a dip belt, or move to rings

Dip workouts and a 6 week plan

Sets with help3 sets of 8 to 12 at your step, 2 minutes of rest
Bar dips3 sets of 8 to 12, with 2 to 3 minutes of rest after hard sets
Dips EMOM10 minutes: at the start of every minute, dips at about half your max, then rest out the minute. Plenty of clean reps and no set to exhaustion
Weighted dips3 to 5 sets of 3 to 6 with a dip belt and 3 to 5 minutes of rest. ACSM advises heavy sets like these once you have trained steadily for about 6 months, and steps of 2 to 10% when you add weight
Max testOne set of clean dips until you cannot do another, every 6 weeks

A 6 week plan.

  • Day 1: test. Warm up, then one set of clean bar dips. Fewer than 8? Pick your step from the five above.
  • Monday, Wednesday and Friday: 3 sets of 8 to 12 at your step, with 2 minutes of rest. Add one rep to each set when every rep was clean. If 8 is too many, go back a step or take more help. At 3 sets of 12, take the next step and go back to 8.
  • End of week 6: test again.

Two or three days a week is what ACSM advises for beginners. For more on rest times, see how long to rest between sets, and for more minute by minute workouts, our guide to the EMOM workout.

Common questions

Are dips better than push-ups?

They train the same muscles: the triceps, the chest and the front of the shoulders. The dip is harder, because you lift nearly all of your weight. A push-up lifts about two thirds to three quarters of it: in a 2014 study, men held 67% of their body weight on their hands at the top of a push-up and 75% at the bottom (Mier and colleagues, 2014). If you cannot do a dip yet, push-ups and the five steps above get you there.

Are bench dips bad for you?

The researchers who compared them say bench dips should not be part of regular training: they took the shoulder past its own range at the bottom and worked the triceps less than bar dips. Triangle push-ups and triceps kickbacks train the triceps without that strain.

Do dips build your chest?

Yes, along with your triceps. In the 2022 study, ring dips worked the chest hardest, then bar dips, and bench dips the least. Whether a bigger forward lean adds more has not been tested on its own.

How often should you do dips?

Two or three days a week, with a day off in between, as ACSM advises for beginners. That is the Monday, Wednesday and Friday of the plan above.

When should you add weight to dips?

When you can do 3 sets of 12 clean bar dips. Hang the weight from a dip belt at your waist, the progression the 2022 researchers suggest, and add a little at a time: ACSM advises steps of 2 to 10%. Extra weight raises the risk of a chest tear, so keep the depth and the grip strict.

Can you do dips at home?

Yes, with a dip station, or dip handles on a power rack. Many outdoor fitness parks have parallel bars too.
