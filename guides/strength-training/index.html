Strength Training Basics

1 guide, with a free PDF

Every workout on this site rests on a few numbers: how many reps, how heavy, how often, and how long to rest between sets. Here they are from the American College of Sports Medicine's guidelines for healthy adults, with our full guide to rest between sets.

Reps and rest by goal

BeginnersA weight you can lift 8 to 12 times a set, and 1 to 2 minutes of rest
MuscleSets of 6 to 12 reps, several of them, with 1.5 to 3 minutes of rest
StrengthHeavy sets of 1 to 6 reps once you have trained for about 6 months, with 2 to 5 minutes of rest
EnduranceLight loads for more than 15 reps, with short rests: 30 seconds to 2 minutes

The reps come from ACSM's guidelines, the rest times from our guide on how long to rest between sets, which adds the newer research.

How often, and when to add weight

  • How often: 2 or 3 days a week when you start, 3 or 4 after about 6 months of steady training, 4 or 5 when advanced.
  • When to add weight: once you can do one or two reps more than your target, raise the load by 2 to 10%.

Source: American College of Sports Medicine. Progression models in resistance training for healthy adults. Medicine and Science in Sports and Exercise, 2009.
