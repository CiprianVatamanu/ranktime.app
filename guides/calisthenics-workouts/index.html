Calisthenics and Bodyweight Workouts

8 guides, each with a free PDF

Calisthenics is training with your own body weight: push-ups, pull-ups, dips, sit-ups, squats and burpees. You need no gym, only a pull-up bar and a pair of dip bars, and every one of these exercises is easy to count, so you always know whether you are getting stronger. These guides cover daily challenges, fitness tests with real standards, and plans that raise your numbers from wherever you start.

Where to start

  • Can't do a pull-up yet? How to do your first pull-up takes you from hangs, rows and slow negatives to your first rep, then to a set of ten.
  • Want a daily habit? 100 push-ups a day or 100 squats a day: 5 to 15 minutes, in as many sets as you need, with a plan for any starting point.
  • Want to know where you stand? Take the push-up test, then see how many pull-ups you should be able to do.
  • Ready for dips? Dips exercise compares bench, bar and ring dips, shows how low to go, and takes you from your first dip to weighted dips in five steps.
  • Wondering about sit-ups? Sit-ups checks six claims against soldier trials and other studies, with the 1 minute test by age and a 5 minute routine.
  • Short on time? Burpee workouts from 4 to 20 minutes, in no more space than your own body length.
