Isometric Exercises: Planks and Wall Sits

2 guides, each with a free PDF

An isometric exercise is a hold: your muscles work hard while your joints stay still, as in a plank or a wall sit. Holds need no equipment and have no impact, and they are among the best studied exercises for blood pressure: in a 2023 review of 270 trials, holds like these lowered blood pressure more than any other kind of training. These guides give you real hold times by age, how to test yourself fairly, and plans to hold longer.
